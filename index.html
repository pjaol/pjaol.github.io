02

Flight path

The short version of how an Irish kid who liked computers ended up building geographic search for one of the biggest sites on the internet, and then a company.

  1. Origin

    Ireland

    Started in university, where it clicked that computing could bridge ideas and opportunity. Never really stopped tinkering after that.

  2. Next hop

    The UK, then the US

    Followed the work across the water, twice. Big systems, bigger teams, and a lot of learning what breaks at scale.

  3. ~ a decade

    AOL

    Large-scale web and search during the years the internet was still figuring itself out. Ranking, relevance, and the endless tug of war between advertising and user satisfaction.

  4. 2007

    LocalLucene

    Built geographic search for Apache Lucene: bounding areas, Cartesian tiers, then a precise distance filter from a center point. AOL contributed it to Apache, it shipped as Spatial Lucene in 2.9, and LocalSolr found its way into Spring, Hibernate, HBase and Compass. NASA JPL folks came knocking about it for ocean and lunar data. Not bad for a filter.

  5. The middle years

    CTO, VP, architect, builder

    Leadership roles across EdTech, HealthTech and startups, including building and managing research and data science orgs. Student evaluation and risk monitoring, conversational patient ML, supply chain analysis, data platforms. The common thread: get the data, make it useful, ship the thing.

  6. 2023

    The VGER Group

    Having worked in small, medium, large and gigantic companies, it was time to build the company I actually wanted to work for. So I did.

  7. Now

    Still shipping

    AI systems for clients, MCP servers, browser extensions, an iOS app and, yes, a font. Same habit as always: if the tool I need doesn't exist, build it well and give it away.
